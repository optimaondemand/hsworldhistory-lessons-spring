Imperialism
Week 30 · Quarter 4
❓ ESSENTIAL QUESTION

What drove European powers to seize or pressure other lands, and how did Africans, Indians, Chinese, and Japanese respond?

🎯 MODULE OBJECTIVES
1
Describe how European powers divided Africa between 1880 and 1900.
2
Explain how Britain came to rule India and how the Raj was governed.
3
Evaluate how technology, rivalry, and African and Indian choices shaped the outcome.
4
Describe how the opium trade led to war and unequal treaties in China.
5
Explain how Commodore Perry's visit set off the Meiji Restoration in Japan.
6
Evaluate why Japan reformed quickly while China struggled to do so.
