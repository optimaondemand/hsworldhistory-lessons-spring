Robert Schuman, Declaration (1950)
The Modern World
💡 BEFORE YOU READ

On May 9, 1950, the French foreign minister read this declaration in Paris. It proposed a pooling of French and German coal and steel and is regarded as the beginning of the European Union. As you read, ask what problem Schuman is trying to solve and what method he proposes.

📜 PRIMARY SOURCE
Robert Schuman, The Schuman Declaration (May 9, 1950)

Europe will not be made all at once, or according to a single plan. It will be built through concrete achievements which first create a de facto solidarity.

The coming together of the nations of Europe requires the elimination of the age-old opposition of France and Germany.

The pooling of coal and steel production will make any war between France and Germany not merely unthinkable, but materially impossible.

The proposal is open to the other countries of Europe that wish to take part.
