35.05 · MODULE MAP
Decolonization and Israel
Week 35 · Quarter 4
❓ ESSENTIAL QUESTION

Why could the European empires not be held after World War II, and what followed in India and in Palestine?

🎯 MODULE OBJECTIVES
1
Explain why European empires in Asia and Africa could not be held after World War II.
2
Describe how India won independence and why it was partitioned.
3
Compare the paths to independence taken in India, Vietnam, Algeria, and Ghana.
4
Describe the origins of Zionism and the competing promises made about Palestine during World War I.
5
Explain how the partition plan of 1947 and the war of 1948 created Israel and a refugee crisis.
6
Summarize the major wars and peace efforts between Israel and its neighbors through the 1990s.
