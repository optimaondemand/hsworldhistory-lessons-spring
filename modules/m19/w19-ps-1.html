💡 BEFORE YOU READ

Bernal Díaz was a common soldier in Cortés's army. He wrote his account some forty years after the events, partly to answer another writer who gave all the credit to the general. As you read, ask what Díaz saw for himself, how he felt about it, and what he had reason to remember or to leave out.
